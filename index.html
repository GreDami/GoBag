FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about go bags, prep kits, and GoBag+.

What is a go bag?

A go bag — also called a bug-out bag or 72-hour kit — is a portable emergency kit with the essentials you need to grab and go during a disaster or evacuation. GoBag+ helps you build one across 9 categories so nothing important is left behind.

What should an emergency prep kit include?

A good prep kit covers water, food, first aid, important documents, tools, shelter, communication, personal care, and cash. GoBag+ organizes all of these and adjusts the recommended quantities to how many people you're preparing for.

How does GoBag+ help me build my go bag and prep kit?

GoBag+ lets you create personalized go bags across 9 categories and track every item, with your readiness shown at a glance. Targets adjust automatically to your household size, so you always know what's stocked and what's still missing.

Can I track home supplies and get expiry alerts?

Yes. Beyond your go bag, GoBag+ tracks your home reserve of food, water, and medicine with minimum stock levels per person, and notifies you automatically before items expire or run low.

Does GoBag+ sync across devices and work for my whole family?

Yes. Your bags and reserve sync across iPhone and iPad via iCloud, and Apple Family Sharing covers up to 5 members on one subscription. You can also keep IDs and passports in a Face ID–protected vault and reach emergency contacts instantly with the SOS button, even offline.

Is GoBag+ free?

GoBag+ is free to download with the core essentials. Premium unlocks unlimited go bags, full home-reserve tracking, family sync, the home-screen widget, backup & restore, and expiry alerts — and starts with a 1-month free trial.
