Features

Key features

Go Bags & Home Reserve

Organize your emergency kits by category and track home supplies with stock levels and expiry dates — all in one place.

Expiry & Low Stock Alerts

Get notified automatically before items expire or fall below your minimum stock level. Never be caught unprepared.

Sync across all iOS devices

Your go bags and reserve stay up to date on every iPhone and iPad you own — automatically, via iCloud.

Family sharing

Share your preparation plan with the whole family. Everyone stays in sync — one subscription covers up to 5 members.

Document Vault

Securely store photos and PDFs of your IDs, passports and insurance cards — protected by Face ID. Accessible only to you.
