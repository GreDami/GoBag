Questions about plans

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. GoBag+ is free to download, and the free plan — one go bag, the core item categories and your emergency contacts — stays free for as long as you use it.

How does the free trial work?

Premium Yearly starts with a free month. No payment is needed for the first 30 days, and you can cancel any time in Settings on your iPhone.

What is the difference between Premium Yearly and Lifetime?

They unlock the same Premium features. Premium Yearly is billed once a year and starts with a free month; Lifetime is a single payment with no subscription, and includes all future updates.

Can my family share one purchase?

Yes. With Apple Family Sharing, Premium Yearly and Lifetime each cover up to 5 family members — no extra purchases and no shared passwords.

Is the price the same in every country?

No. The App Store sets the price for each region and charges it in your local currency, and it shows you the exact amount before you confirm a purchase.
