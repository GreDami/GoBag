How to use GoBag+

Set up your go bag, your home reserve, your emergency contacts and your document vault in about ten minutes, screen by screen.

Short answer

Download GoBag+ from the App Store and every feature is free for the first month, with no card. Open your go bag, which comes sorted into nine categories, and tick off what you already have; set the size of your household in the home reserve so minimum stock levels follow; add your emergency contacts to the SOS button; and put copies of your IDs in the Face ID vault. After the free month, Premium Yearly or Lifetime keeps the bags, the reserve and the alerts going, and your emergency contacts stay free.

GoBag+ turns the checklists in these guides into something you can keep up to date: a go bag for each person, a home reserve sized to your household, your emergency numbers and copies of your documents, all on your iPhone or iPad. Here is how to set it up, in the order that takes the least time.

1. Download and start your free month

GoBag+ is free to download on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Your first month is free too: every feature, no card needed, nothing to cancel. Open the app and you land on the home screen, with four buttons: the go bag, the SOS button, the document vault and the home reserve.

2. Build your go bag

Tap the backpack. Your go bag is already sorted into nine categories: Documents, Water & Food, Clothing, Medical & Hygiene, Tools & Equipment, Electronics, Money, Personal & Family and Miscellaneous. Open a category, mark what is already packed and add what you still need; the ring around each category, and the big one at the top, show how complete the bag is. If something does not fit a category, add one of your own.

With Premium you can keep up to five go bags, one for each person or car. Not sure what to pack? The go bag checklist goes category by category.

3. Set up your home reserve

Tap the house. The app first asks how many people you are preparing household supplies for; the recommended minimum stock levels follow that number, and change if your household does. The reserve is sorted into Water & Food, Medical & Hygiene, Electronics, Cleaning, Fuel & Energy, Tools & Equipment and Other.

Enter what you have, and the screen shows how well stocked you are, which items are running low and which have no level set yet. The water and food guide explains how much a household needs.

4. Add expiry dates and turn on alerts

Give water, food, medicine and batteries their expiry date when you add them. The bag then flags what has expired and what is about to, and with Premium you get a notification before anything expires or drops below its minimum, so the six-month check takes minutes instead of an afternoon.

5. Add your emergency contacts

Tap SOS and add the people you would call: family, your doctor, the authorities and anyone else, each in its own group. They stay one tap away, even offline. Your emergency contacts remain free after the trial, on any plan.

6. Put your documents in the vault

Tap the document with the lock, then the plus button, and add photos or PDFs of your passports, IDs and insurance cards. The vault opens only with Face ID, and your files stay on your device; when they sync, they travel through Apple's encrypted iCloud. The emergency documents guide lists what is worth copying.

7. Add the widget

With Premium, the home-screen widget shows your go bag and home reserve levels side by side, so you see how ready you are every time you unlock your phone. Touch and hold the home screen, tap the add button and choose GoBag+.

8. Share with your family

With Premium, your bags and reserve sync across every iPhone and iPad you own through iCloud, and Apple Family Sharing covers up to five members on one subscription. Everyone sees the same bags, so whoever does the shopping knows what is missing. Agree on the rest in your family emergency plan.

9. Back up and choose a plan

You can always make a backup of everything you entered, on any plan; restoring from a backup is part of Premium. When the free month ends, choose Premium Yearly or a one-time Lifetime purchase to keep your go bags, home reserve, family sync, widget and alerts. The pricing page compares the plans.

Ready in ten minutes. Download GoBag+, open your go bag and tick off what you already have. Most people find the bag is further along than they thought, and the app shows exactly what is left.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoBag+ free?

GoBag+ is free to download, and your first month is free with every feature and no card. After that, Premium Yearly or Lifetime keeps your go bags, home reserve, family sync, widget and expiry alerts going; your emergency contacts stay free.

Does GoBag+ work offline?

Yes. Your data lives on your device, and your emergency contacts can be reached with one tap even without a connection.

Can my family share the same go bags?

Yes, with Premium. Bags and the home reserve sync through iCloud across your iPhone and iPad, and Apple Family Sharing covers up to five members on one subscription.

Where does GoBag+ store my documents?

On your device, in a vault that opens only with Face ID. When files sync they travel through Apple's encrypted iCloud; GreDami never sees them, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

Does GoBag+ work on Android?

No, GoBag+ is for iPhone and iPad, on the App Store.
